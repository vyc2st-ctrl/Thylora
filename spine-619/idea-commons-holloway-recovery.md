## Context: Vashon High School

Vashon High School opened in St. Louis on 11 September 1927. It was the city's second high school for Black students. It is named for two African American educators:

- **George Boyer Vashon** (1824–1878), the first Black graduate of Oberlin College (1844).
- **John Boyer Vashon**, his son, a St. Louis teacher and principal for 34 years.

## Connected Earth question

**Before you pay an invention company, what must they tell you?**

Since the American Inventors Protection Act of 1999, 35 U.S.C. § 297 requires an invention promoter to disclose in writing, before a contract, its five-year record:

- how many inventions it evaluated, and how many evaluations were positive or negative;
- how many customers it had;
- how many of those customers made a net profit from its services.

Injured customers can sue for statutory damages of up to $5,000 or for actual damages, and up to triple for willful violations. The USPTO publishes complaints against promoters.
