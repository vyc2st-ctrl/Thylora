# Living Scene Passport: No-Image Digital Spec (PROTOTYPE)

- Record: `THY-LSP-SPEC-619`
- Revenue path: `REV-618-LIVING-SCENE-001` (NOT_LIVE)
- Idea: `THY-IDEA-LIVING-SCENE-CLOCK-WITNESS-001`
- Source state: `THY-SCENE-001-DOCTOR-LEDGER-619`

**What it is.** A one-scene reading packet that shows how a scene is *known* before it is drawn. The buyer or learner sees the full ledger behind a single moment:

- the clocks;
- who was there and why;
- where the vehicles stood;
- who witnessed what;
- which facts are still UNKNOWN.

The packet then asks the learner to find the next missing fact. It needs no image, so it is not blocked by the visual gate.

## Pages (8)

1. **The moment.** One paragraph in plain words: a doctor arrives at night in hard rain.
2. **Four clocks.** World, Earth (inactive, with the reason why), scene beats t0 to t0+14 s, and the witness clock.
3. **People first.** A person card for each visible person. Only known fields are filled. UNKNOWN is printed as UNKNOWN. Unnamed slots stay unnamed.
4. **Place and geometry.** Bay, doors, clear lane, walk and portico. Sourced values and proposed values are marked differently.
5. **Vehicles and objects.** What is known, and the list of what is not.
6. **Witness map.** Who could see what, from where. Cameras appear only if they exist.
7. **Contradiction page.** The "25 covered feet" versus umbrella-in-rain conflict. The learner decides which evidence wins and why.
8. **Earth bridge.** "What should a doctor know before the call begins?", with a one-line pointer to the learner's own local health service. No medical advice.

## Learning use

POV exercises from the same state engine: driver's seat, doorway, and (later) bird's-eye or neighbor-camera views. Each learner rewrites the moment from one witness without adding facts.
