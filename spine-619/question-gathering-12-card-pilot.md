# Question Gathering: 12-Card Pilot (DRAFT)

**Purpose.** Help a family, class or group test an answer they *already agree with*. Nobody has to join a belief. Every card works the same way on a mainstream claim and on an alternative one, including claims about history, ancestry and lineage that have been suppressed or under-reported. In both cases the cards separate what is known from what is guessed and what is still unknown.

**Safeguards (printed on the box insert).**
- No card asks anyone to share private family, health, money or legal details.
- Children play with an adult present. Any answer can be "I'd rather not say."
- The deck gives no verdicts. It hands out questions.

Each card carries its QYRIS step: Question, Yield, Reason, Inspect, Safeguard.

| # | QYRIS | Card question | Try it |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Question | What do I already believe about this, and when did I first hear it? | Write down the belief and your earliest memory of hearing it. |
| 2 | Question | Who told me, and how did *they* know? | Trace one step back from the person who told you. |
| 3 | Yield | What would I expect to see if I were wrong? | Name one thing you could actually go and look for. |
| 4 | Yield | What is the strongest form of the answer I disagree with? | Say it well enough that someone who holds it would nod. |
| 5 | Reason | What evidence would change my mind, and have I looked for it? | Set a search you will actually do. |
| 6 | Reason | Who is missing from this story, and what would they say? | Name a group whose records were lost, kept out or never written down. |
| 7 | Inspect | Which part is known, which part is guessed, and which part is still UNKNOWN? | Sort the claim into three piles. |
| 8 | Inspect | Who benefits if everyone believes this, and who pays? | List both sides, then check whether "who benefits" proves anything by itself. |
| 9 | Inspect | Is the source a first-hand record, a later retelling, or an opinion? | Label each source you used. |
| 10 | Safeguard | Could this answer hurt someone if we're wrong? | Decide what you would *not* say publicly yet. |
| 11 | Safeguard | What is the smallest honest test we could do this week? | Pick one step and one date. |
| 12 | Next question | What should the next person ask after us? | Hand the card on with your question written on the back. |

**Worked example for the facilitator guide.** Card 6 applied to a school history unit: "Whose records survive about this period, and whose were destroyed, never written, or kept in another language?" The answer points to archives, oral history and the record-loss gaps. It does not point to a conclusion chosen in advance.
