What are you trying to understand, preserve, or build?

THYLORA is not a chat box and not a single product. It is a working method with a backend behind it: every claim is filed with its evidence, every gap is filed as a gap, and UNKNOWN stays UNKNOWN.

Everything listed below is marked with the state of its evidence. Nothing on this page is sold, activated, or claimed as a live service unless the backend can show the record.

Three doors

Pick the one that matches your problem.

DOOR ONE

Understand it

You have a claim, a number, a quote, a document or a story and you need to know what it actually supports.

  • Sharpen a vague question into one that can be answered
  • Map what is known, what is evidenced, and what is only asserted
  • Test a historical claim against what its source really says
  • Read an equation as a sentence before calculating anything
DOOR TWO

Preserve it

You have something that will be lost if nobody records it properly — a family story, an object, a line of work.

  • Record a told story with its teller, its date and its confidence level
  • Keep interpretation separate from what was actually said
  • Hold consent and visibility as fields, not as an afterthought
  • Keep a record's history when it changes instead of overwriting it
DOOR THREE

Build it

You have a project, a world, a product line or a system and it keeps drifting, contradicting itself, or stalling.

  • Give a project one restart point that survives an interruption
  • Name the blockers and the tests that say a thing is finished
  • Keep a story world internally consistent across many episodes
  • Separate lanes so one project cannot quietly contaminate another

The question function · MATH-Q-001 · ACTIVE

Q = f(K, E, C)

The question you are able to ask improves as knowledge, evidence and connections improve. A vague question is usually not a thinking failure — it is a signal that one of the three inputs is thin.

K
what is known
E
what is evidenced
C
what connects to what
f
the relationship between them
Q
the question you can now ask

Use it when a problem feels vague: identify what is known, what is evidenced and what connects, before asking the next question.

ACCESS ≠ AUTHORITY Reaching a system and being allowed to act in it are two different things. THYLORA holds this as an enforced gate, not a slogan: a process can have full read and write access to the backend and still have no authority to publish, to spend, to release, or to speak for anyone. This page was built by a process with write access to the backend and no authority to publish anything — so nothing here was published.

Real examples

Four questions THYLORA has actually run.

These are THYLORA's own records. There are no customer testimonials on this page because there is no customer story THYLORA has consent to tell.

"Who built Windsor Castle in stone?"

The common answer names William I. The record does not support it. What is documented is that the c.1070 work was earth and timber; the first documented stone is under Henry II; the silhouette people picture today is Edward III plus a nineteenth-century remodelling. THYLORA had previously carried the overstatement itself, and the correction is filed as a correction rather than quietly swapped in.

Ten-row evidence matrix, four evidence classes · rule THY-HIST-METHOD-001, "construction phase is not technology origin"

"Is the 364-day calendar in the Dead Sea Scrolls solved?"

Two different things get merged here. In weeks it is exactly stable — 364 ÷ 7 = 52, so every date falls on the same weekday every year, and that part is arithmetically certain. Against the sun it is not stable at all: 365.2422 − 364 = 1.2422 days of drift per year. "Solved" and "found" are not the same claim, and the arithmetic is separated from what any source asserts.

Finding FIND-QUMRAN-364-DRIFT-566 · truth class ARITHMETIC_CERTAIN_PLUS_SOURCE_CLAIM

"My AI assistant can act for me now. What is it allowed to do?"

Almost all current reporting measures access — what an agent can reach. Very little measures authority — what it is permitted to decide. THYLORA separates the two in its own tables and can show the rows: a process here holds full backend write access and zero publishing authority, and the gate is what stops it, not good intentions.

Gate LAW-ACCESS-NOT-AUTHORITY-001 · state IMPLEMENTED

"Is this famous quote real?"

Widely circulated quotations fail primary-source checks at a high rate. So a quote is scored before it is used at all: can the source be verified, is the attribution right, is the context preserved, and does it turn into a real question. A quote that cannot clear all four does not become a post — it becomes a recorded unknown.

F = S × A × C × T, registered ACTIVE · 23 quotes scored, lowest 320, highest 625
