# THYLORA Brand Rules

Status: DRAFT, for Chairman review · 3 October 2026

## 1 · The mark: ?V

![?V mark](question-v-mark.svg)

A question mark with a **V where the period would be**. The dot of a question mark
closes the question. The V keeps it open and points down to where the answer
lands. The question is never finished. It keeps moving.

| Rule | Detail |
|---|---|
| Shape | One stroke for the hook. The V sits under it with its point on the baseline. Never a dot. |
| Minimum size | 6 mm tall on fabric, 4 mm in print. Below that the V fills in and reads as a dot. |
| Color | One color only. Default black on light, white on dark. Simulation material: red `#B00012`. |
| Where it goes | **Every product.** On hats it can be small (side or back), but it is always there. |
| Simulation seal | The red seal carries the mark. The seal says *not an Earth government seal*. That wording stays. |

## 2 · An equation on everything

Every product carries one equation, written the way a classroom board shows it:

1. the expression by itself
2. a key to every letter, set apart from the expression
3. *the question this expression asks*

If a product is too small for the key (a hat brim), print the expression and put
the key and the question on the tag or a QR card. Never print a letter that nobody
has defined.

Starter set:

| Expression | The question it asks |
|---|---|
| ΔQ = Q<sub>after</sub> − Q<sub>before</sub> | How far did it move? |
| PV = A × T × U × R | Is it accurate, timely, usable and trusted, all at once? |
| C = H × Q × K | Did it arrive whole, answered and with one meaning? |

## 4 · How to sell it without breaking the commerce canon

The deployment repo's `THYLORA_COMMERCE_PROVIDER_CANON.md` already says:
**Stripe takes payment. Print fulfillment is a separate partner.** So:

- **Payment:** Stripe (already approved).
- **Making and shipping:** a print-on-demand partner (Printful, Printify or similar)
  makes each item when it is ordered. No inventory.
- **Shopify** is optional. It is not on the approved list today. Adding it is a
  Chairman decision, not a default.

## 5 · The tattoo (personal, not merch)

| Item | Spec |
|---|---|
| Design | The ?V mark, plus the chosen phrase. **Phrase: CONFIRM.** It came from a photo that did not reach this session. |
| Placement | Neck, just behind and below the **left** ear |
| Size | About 2.5 to 3.5 cm tall. Ask the artist about line spread at that size; the V gap must stay open. |
| Color | Choose against your skin tone in daylight. Black and deep red hold longest. White and light colors fade fastest on darker skin. |
| Before | Print the SVG at actual size, cut it out, tape it in place and live with it for a week. |

## 7 · Printing at home: save the ink

Set the printer default to **black and white / grayscale** for drafts. The court
packet background is a light line drawing, not a solid fill, so it uses very
little toner. Only the red simulation boxes need color; print those pages in color
and the rest in grayscale.
