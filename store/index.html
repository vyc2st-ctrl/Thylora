Delivery and re-access, one rule for the lane

Delivery
Store order → entitlement written against the order → the packet opens at its product path. Print or save as PDF from inside the packet. No attachment to lose, no download link to expire.
Re-access
Perpetual. A purchase entitlement, not a subscription: cancellation cannot revoke it, and it is not limited to one device or one download.
Corrections
Issued as a new edition. An existing entitlement opens the new edition at no cost.
Serial
Identifies the copy, not the holder. Carries no personal detail. Preview copies are stamped 0000.
