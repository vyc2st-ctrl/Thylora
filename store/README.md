## Quality floor for this lane

Refused: plain white PDFs, generic worksheets, stock self-help language,
fake testimonials, invented statistics, outcome promises, urgency and scarcity
devices, and strike-through pricing against a price never charged.

Required: designed pages, visual reasoning, a worked example filled in end to
end, plain/everyday/technical explanations of every move, real utility, and a
clean fillable final page.
