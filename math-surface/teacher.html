WHAT THIS CHANGES ON MONDAY

A mark on a word problem is one number covering three questions.

This page turns that one number back into three, refuses the claims the evidence does not support, and tells you which layer is worth your next hour.

Admissible evidence

A layer counts as measured only when it was probed in isolation — when the other two layers could not have caused the failure.

LayerAn isolated probe looks likeIt is not isolated if
L LanguageThe learner says what the sentence is telling us and what it is asking for. No numbers, no operators.the probe contains a calculation the learner could get wrong
M RelationshipThe quantities are handed over already extracted. The learner names, points at or draws the relationship. Nothing is computed.the learner still has to find the numbers in a sentence
S SolveBare computation. No story, no words.there is a context to interpret

A score from the mixed word problem is recorded, but it is not evidence about any single layer. The model carries that distinction as a field, not as a footnote.

The claim the model will refuse

This learner has no isolated measurement of the relationship layer. No statement about their mathematics is available from this sitting.

If L = 0, P_solve = 0 — and that zero is equally consistent with perfect mathematics and with none. Reading it as a mathematics result is the single most expensive mistake available here, because it buys a year of the wrong intervention.
