Edition Preview for Approval

Policy THY-APPROVAL-MUST-BE-VIEWABLE-001. Nothing on this page asks for a decision without first showing the thing being decided on.

What this surface does, and what it refuses to do

  • It shows the artifact. Cover and every text block, in order, exactly as stored.
  • It binds what you see. Each edition carries its package version, its stored source hash, and a SHA-256 digest computed server-side over the exact content rendered.
  • It refuses a stale decision. If the content changes between your reading it and your deciding, the database rejects the write rather than attaching your name to something else.
  • It does not publish. There is no publish call on this page. APPROVE records a finding; it does not release, activate or schedule anything. Publishing remains the separate, validator-gated act on the Store Release panel.
  • It does not overwrite history. Decisions are appended to an immutable table. UPDATE and DELETE are revoked and trigger-blocked.
