NEED HELP? TELL US WHERE THE NEXT STEP BREAKS.

Email ersatzrealityenterprise@gmail.com. Send your country, city or region, preferred language, what you need, and whether a deadline is close. If you cannot call, write “email only.” If someone you know needs help, ask them to write to us or get their permission before sharing their story.

No U.S. hotline works everywhere. We will look for the relevant service *where you are*, show you the source and date, and help you form the next question. Then tell us what happened. If that door did not open, we keep looking for another route with you.

Need to post or sell something? Tell us what you make and where your post stalls. We can review the message and explore a digital product or store partnership together. No listing or money split happens until ownership, consent, price, costs and exactly who gets paid are agreed in writing.

Please do not send medical records, ID numbers, account details, court papers, or your exact sleeping location in the first email. We review messages manually; this is not an emergency service and no response time is guaranteed. If you need help right now, seek an established local emergency or aid service.
