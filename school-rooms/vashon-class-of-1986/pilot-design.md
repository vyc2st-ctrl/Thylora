## 3 · Memory archive rules (PERSON · PRIVACY · RIGHTS)
- **Faces:** a photo is public inside the room only when every identifiable person has consented, is the contributor, or has been blurred. Otherwise it is held.
- **Classmates who have died:** a memorial page only at the request or with the consent of family. It never uses health or cause-of-death details unless family provides them for that purpose.
- **Yearbook scans:** the yearbook's copyright holder is UNKNOWN (often the school or the publisher). **RIGHTS HOLD** on yearbook reproduction. Contributors' own snapshots are fine with consent.
- **Documented vs remembered:** stories are welcome and labelled as memory. Claims about named people carry the PERSON gate.
- **Takedown:** anyone pictured can request removal; it is honoured within a stated time (to be set).

## 4 · Students and mentorship (LEGAL ESCALATE: minors)
- There are **no student accounts until LEGAL review.** Phase 1 student access runs through a **teacher sponsor** who posts class questions on the students' behalf.
- **No private channels between adults and students.** All mentorship happens in visible, moderated threads or in school-hosted sessions.
- Mentors are screened before any direct involvement with students. The screening standard is UNKNOWN and set by LEGAL and the school.
- No student data beyond first name and grade is collected, and only if the school agrees.

## 5 · Global school replication kit
1. **Room template:** the same 10 rooms, renamed per school.
2. **Founding steward role:** one alum per class year.
3. **Gate path:** PERSON → PRIVACY → RIGHTS → LEGAL → PRODUCT → MONEY → STORE → PUBLICATION. Each new school starts at PERSON.
4. **Supporter price rule:** the class year's two-digit number divided by 10 (for example '86 → $8.60, '92 → $9.20). This is PROPOSED, and it is a naming pattern, not an approved price.
5. **Localization:** student-privacy law is per country and per state. The kit ships per jurisdiction only after LEGAL review.
