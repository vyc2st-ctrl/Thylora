What this surface is

The witness surface for the R6 lane. The same modules load into the authoritative dashboard through one added script tag; this page exists so the lane can be opened, driven and witnessed on iPad on its own, without the rest of the head in the way.

Readback needs a real user gesture before iPadOS will speak. Press Open, then Speak.

Witness sequence — iPad first

  1. Open the workspace. Paste or load a response into the left lane, press Load response.
  2. Press Speak. The spoken words highlight in place; read-ahead shows what is still coming.
  3. Press Pause, then Resume — it continues mid-sentence, not from the top.
  4. Press ↺ 10s and 10s ↻ during readback. Drag the speed slider while it speaks.
  5. Hold the microphone and speak. The readback ducks; the transcript builds; on release the note lands with its anchor percentage.
  6. Select two notes, press BUILD NEXT PROMPT FROM SELECTED NOTES.
  7. Draw on the canvas with Apple Pencil. Rest your palm on the glass — it must not draw.
  8. Preview room: open an image, enlarge it, mark it up, drop a pin, approve.
  9. Coverage ledger: atomize a prompt, resolve atoms, try DEFERRED_WITH_REASON with no reason — it must refuse.
  10. Arrival matrix and money-distance: load from backend and read what it reports.
