## Tonight: Cali's roast game

**Your equation:** R = O × T × B × C. The letter meanings below are **proposed**; you confirm them.
- **O** = Observation (something true you noticed about the person)
- **T** = Timing (say it at the right moment)
- **B** = **Bond** (you love the person you're roasting)
- **C** = Cleverness (twist it so it's funny, not just mean)

**B = 0 → R = 0.** With no love behind it, it isn't a roast, it's just hurting someone. That is what happened with Jordan. Because everything is multiplied, one zero zeroes the whole thing.

**Name options** (to replace "roast lab"): **Cali's Crown Court** · **Bus Stop Bout** · **The Heat Table** · **Flame & Fam**

**Game rules (family, bus stop, anywhere):**
1. Three rounds. Each person gets one roast per round.
2. Only roast what a person **chose**: shoes, habits, how long they take in the bathroom. Never their body, family losses, or anything they can't change.
3. It has to be **true** (O) or it doesn't count.
4. After every roast, the roaster gives one real compliment. That keeps B above zero.
5. The crowd claps once for each letter the roast hit (O, T, B, C). Four claps is a perfect roast.
6. Nobody takes sides. You judge the roast, not the person.

**How Cali builds one:** notice something, find the funny truth in it, stretch it bigger, then say it in under 10 words.
> Example: "Diamond takes so long to get ready, the bus filed a missing-person report."
