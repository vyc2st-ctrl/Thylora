## 3 · The anatomy model (the spine question)

The Chairman asked: what is a sequence, how much does it hold, what are the gates,
and what comes before the brain. Proposed model — to be confirmed by the Chairman:

| Body part | THYLORA part | Behaviour |
|---|---|---|
| **Vertebra** | A **sequence** (e.g. 624) | A numbered segment of the record. It does not "fill up" — it holds as many entries as are added. Staying on 624 is fine *as long as it keeps accepting entries*. A new number only when a new era/phase begins. |
| **Cells** | Each **idea** | Every idea gets its **own ID** (e.g. `IDEA-624-031`) attached to a sequence. Ideas are not sequences; they live inside one. |
| **Ligaments** (bone ↔ bone) | **Connections between ideas** | The recall registry. Holds "the Printer evidence connects to the store"; "the lemonade connects to Cali's recipe book." Tight on purpose. |
| **Tendons** (muscle ↔ bone) | **Ideas ↔ the people/departments that move them** | Every idea gets an owner. An idea with no tendon cannot move — this is why the store is slow. |
| **Vessels / veins** | **Routing** | How work and messages travel between departments, assistants, dashboards. |
| **Immune system** | **Gates** | Detect → decide → defend → **remember**. Immune memory = a gate that learns: once a bad pattern is caught, every gate knows it. Gates don't only block — they direct (white cells go *to* the problem). |
| **Brain** | Chairman logic | Thinking, choosing. |
| **Before the brain** | **Lineage / root** | Values that existed before any decision — the ancestral layer. (See §9: the Yoruba idea of *Orí*, chosen before birth.) This is the part the Chairman sees but couldn't name. |

**Sweet-potato note (the Chairman's own evidence):** the "strings" were roots. Every product
needs its root visible — where it came from, who it connects to. That is what "the world
remembering itself" points at: every part carries the memory of its root.

**Ledger lag fix:** "some of the ledger is behind the new ones" happens when work is created
first and recorded later. Rule: **ledger-first. No entry, no existence.** An idea gets its ID
*before* any work starts; a product gets its passport *before* it's listed.

## 10 · Catch-me notes (truth checks, as requested)

- **ADHD "all Black kids":** the record doesn't support that claim. A large US study (Morgan et al.,
  *Pediatrics*, 2013) found Black children were *less* likely than white children with the same
  symptoms to be diagnosed. The documented bias is elsewhere: Black students are suspended
  ~3× as often (US Dept. of Education civil-rights data), and medicine has a real history of
  pathologising Black behaviour — *drapetomania* (Cartwright, 1851, "the disease" of wanting freedom)
  and 1960s "protest psychosis" schizophrenia diagnoses (Metzl, *The Protest Psychosis*).
  The Chairman's point about **no benchmark for "too excited"** stands — that subjectivity is where bias enters.
- **Turkey sleepiness:** turkey's tryptophan is about the same as chicken's. The drowsiness is mostly
  meal size, carbs and alcohol. The drowsy-driving risk after big holiday meals is real — that's the report worth making.
- **Honey that smells like syrup:** honey adulteration with sugar syrups is a documented global problem
  (an EU check in 2021–22 flagged ~46% of sampled imported honey as suspicious). Buy raw honey from a
  known local beekeeper.
- **Live-bet math (pothole question):** at −4000, $800 wins $20 — correct. Question for the Chairman:
  *how many $20 wins does one loss erase?* (Forty.) Breakeven at −4000 is a **97.6%** hit rate.

## 11 · Food (Key)

**Chobani ingredients, plainly:**
- Cultured low-fat milk — the yogurt itself.
- Cane sugar — added sugar; the main long-term concern.
- Natural flavors — legal term; source is natural but the recipe isn't disclosed.
- Sea salt — flavour.
- Fruit/vegetable extract or juice concentrate — usually colour/flavour.
- Guar gum, locust bean gum — plant thickeners (guar bean, carob seed); generally safe; some get gas.
- Lemon juice concentrate — lemon juice with water removed for shelf life; same acid, more consistent.
- Lactase — an enzyme that pre-digests milk sugar so it's easier on lactose-sensitive people (and tastes slightly sweeter).
- *S. thermophilus, L. bulgaricus* (classic yogurt cultures) + *L. acidophilus, Bifidus, L. casei, L. rhamnosus* (probiotics).
- **Long term:** nothing alarming; the added sugar is what to watch.
- **Fewest ingredients:** plain yogurt whose label reads only *milk (and cream), live cultures* — e.g. FAGE Total plain. Add your own fruit/honey.

cucumbers, tomatoes, onions, plain yogurt (check label), plus 2–3 new things: mango, kiwi, pomegranate,
bell peppers, zucchini, spinach. Portions small — 4–5 pineapple pieces, halved grapes.
