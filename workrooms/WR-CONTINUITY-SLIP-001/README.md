The words must enter the backend at the point where you type them, not after an AI reads them.

- **Already built:** your dashboard's **Command** box saves your exact text into `thylora_chairman_commands.command_text` before any AI touches it. There are 114 rows, the last on 2026-09-05.

**Precheck to make permanent (held):** a custody row counts as captured only when all three hold:
- `verbatim_locked = true`
- the stored text's sha256 equals the source hash
- `length(user_message) > 0`

A `STRUCTURED_RECOVERY` row stays **OPEN, not complete**, until the words arrive. This turns S1 from a silent loss into a visible blocker.
