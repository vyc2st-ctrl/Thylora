## 1 · LOCKED RULE: Equation Six-Step Lock (proposed as THY-LAW-EQUATION-SIX-STEP-639)

Every equation on a PDF, shirt, card, post, EDF or report shows, **in this order**:

1. **SEE IT**: the whole formula, large, first, as an actual equation.
2. **NAME EVERY PART**: each symbol with its meaning and unit, right beside it.
3. **SAY IT**: one sentence, plus how to read it aloud.
4. **SWAP IT**: everyday things in the letters (P = potatoes, S = salad) to show the shape still works.
5. **WORK IT**: real numbers put in.
6. **ASK IT**: the question the equation answers.

Plus: **7 · PRINT-PROVE IT**. Fonts are embedded and the glyph check passes before any file ships.
The aim: after seeing it six ways, a person reads the symbols the way they read 2 × 2.

This **enhances** MATH-DISPLAY-CLOSURE-625 and rule I1 (question under every equation). It conflicts with neither.

## 3 · The gap between deduction and conclusion

**Deduction** answers: *"If these premises are true, what must follow?"*
**Conclusion** is what we then *say is true, and act on.*

The gap is three silent jumps:

| Jump | What gets skipped | THYLORA check |
|---|---|---|
| ① Premise truth | Deduction proves only "IF premises, THEN conclusion." It never proves the premises. | Label each premise CANON / EARTH / INFERENCE / UNKNOWN (`MATH-POSITION-EPISTEMIC-601`) |
| ② Scope | A rule true *in general* gets applied to *this* person, place or time | `MATH-CONTEXTUAL-GAP-622`: does the context match? |
| ③ Readback | "It follows" gets treated as "it happened." Nobody checked the world. | `MATH-REVERIFY-593` + `MATH-VERIFIED-ADVANCE-637` (who verified, who cleared) |

As an equation (proposed **MATH-DEDUCTION-GAP-639**):

**C_safe = V × T_p × S × R**

| Symbol | Meaning | Scale |
|---|---|---|
| C_safe | permission to state the conclusion as true | 0 = HOLD |
| V | the logic is valid (it really follows) | 0/1 |
| T_p | every premise is verified, not assumed | 0/1 |
| S | the scope fits this case | 0/1 |
| R | read back against the real world | 0/1 |

**Say it:** a conclusion is safe only when the logic follows, the starting facts are true, the rule fits this case, and we checked.
**Swap it:** "All stores close at 9 (premise) → the corner store is closed now (deduction)." But is it 9? Is the corner store one of "all stores"? Did anyone look?
**Ask it:** *What did I assume that I never checked?*

You found this gap in 637 when you said "who verified it could advance, what cleared it." This equation is that same gap, located one step earlier: at the conclusion, not the action.

## 4 · Other ways of logic (not your logic: different ones)

| # | Way | The question it asks | When it beats the others |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Deductive | What must follow? | Rules are certain |
| 2 | Inductive | What usually happens? | Many examples, no rule yet |
| 3 | Abductive | What best explains this? | Detective work, diagnosis |
| 4 | Analogical | What is this like? | New problem, old pattern |
| 5 | Counterfactual | What if that hadn't happened? | Finding the cause |
| 6 | Dialectical | What is the strongest opposite? | Arguments, courts |
| 7 | Systems / relational | What is connected to what? | Ubuntu-style: a person is a person through others |
| 8 | Paraconsistent | Can two conflicting records both stay without everything collapsing? | Archives, oral and written history disagreeing |
| 9 | Fuzzy | How true, 0 to 1? | Real life is rarely yes/no |
| 10 | Bayesian | How much should this evidence change my mind? | Updating beliefs |
| 11 | Catuṣkoṭi (Indian four-corner) | Is it true, false, both, or neither? | Questions with no clean answer |
| 12 | Ifá binary divination logic (Yoruba) | Which of 256 combined patterns fits? | A binary system with 2⁸ = 256 odù, centuries old (EARTH; source check before publishing) |
| 13 | Erotetic (logic of questions) | What answers does this question allow? | Writing better questions: your lane |

**Different ways to ask the same question** (example: *"Why is the store not selling?"*):
- Before-A: what had to be true before anyone would buy?
- Inversion: how would we make sure *nobody* buys?
- Smallest test: what one change can we measure by Friday?
- Outsider: what would a 9-year-old say is confusing?
- Evidence: who saw it, from where, and how sure are they?
- Future-regret: what will we wish we had measured today?
