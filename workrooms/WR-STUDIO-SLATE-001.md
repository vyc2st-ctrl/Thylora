## 0 · Earth material: what we may use and what we may not

| We may | We may not |
|---|---|
| Adapt **public-domain** works: *Frankenstein* (Shelley, 1818), *Dracula* (Stoker, 1897), *Carmilla* (Le Fanu, 1872), *Dr Jekyll and Mr Hyde* (Stevenson, 1886), *The Legend of Sleepy Hollow* (Irving, 1820), *The Invisible Man* (Wells, 1897), Grimm and Aesop tales, Br'er Rabbit and other African American folk tales as collected before 1930 | Copy a copyrighted film, comic, character, logo or score (e.g. a studio's specific film version, a Marvel/DC hero) |
| Study how genres work (heist structure, monster-movie pacing, three-act screenplay) and build our own | Present our work as if it were an Earth company's work |
| Credit Earth facts and history with sources | Invent history and present it as fact |

Rule: THYLORA originals look like THYLORA, not like a copy of Earth.

## 1 · Comics (four lines, twelve titles)

### Line A · Heroes (all ages)
1. ***The Brick Wall*** — Junie Calloway's mirror: a records clerk who can walk into any document and stand inside the year it was written. Villain: **The Burner**, who erases courthouses.
2. ***Porch Light*** — Mara Vale (from *Bramble Wick*) grows up; her lantern shows the true outline of anything it touches, lies included.
3. ***Hatchie Bend Irregulars*** — the Tolliver kids' bike crew; each issue solves one mystery with one real science fact.

### Line B · Superheroes
4. ***Sixty*** — a girl born in the Sixty Census House reads every name ever written in it; each name she says out loud gives her one ancestor's skill for one hour.
5. ***Ironside Brickport*** — a steelworker in the mirror St. Louis whose body takes on the strength of every family that came north on the same train.
6. ***The Teddy*** — comic relief hero. Can make anyone laugh at the worst moment, which turns out to be a superpower. (Inspired by the Chairman's Uncle Teddy; not a portrait of him.)

### Line C · Horror (adults)
7. ***Dracula of the Delta*** — public-domain *Dracula*, moved to a river-bottom plantation in 1866: the count arrives to buy the land after the war, and the freedpeople are the ones who know what he is.
8. ***The Stitched Man*** — *Frankenstein* told by the creature, built by a doctor from the bodies of the unclaimed; he goes looking for whose hands he has.
9. ***Carmilla Comes to Brickport*** — 1950s Great Migration city; a boarding-house lodger who never eats.

### Line D · History (true stories, cited)
10. ***1870*** — the first census to name formerly enslaved people, one family per issue.
11. ***Booker T.'s Namesakes*** — real people named after Booker T. Washington, and why their parents chose it.
12. ***The Road From Mason*** — the Peete · Wright case as a graphic history.

## 2 · Cards (five types, none of them a standard Earth deck)

| Card type | What's on it | How it's played or used |
|---|---|---|
| **Case Cards** | One proven ancestor: name, years, county, the record that proves it | *Kinfolk Klaim* game; LEAD cards (dashed border) can't be played as proof |
| **Brick Wall Cards** | One record type (slave schedule, will, Freedman's Bank card) with how to find it | The "key" cards that break a Brick Wall in the game |
| **Mirror Cards** | Pairs: an Earth family moment and its THYLORA mirror | Memory/match game; families make their own pairs |
| **Math Path Cards** | One number from a real story, with the full working (e.g. 8 May 1936 → 7 Sep 1990 = 54 years, 3 months, 30 days) | Kids' learning deck: every card is a word problem from a true record |
| **Porch Cards** | One family saying + who said it + when ("Get cho one-legged self out here" — Uncle Teddy) | Story-starter deck; draw one, tell the story behind it |

## 5 · Logo department brief

Each line above gets its own mark. The brief to the designers is the same for
every one: **one shape that says what the line does, readable at 16 pixels,
no borrowed Earth symbols.**
- Lineage Desk: a porch rocker whose runners are two lines of a family tree.
- Brick Wall comics: one brick with a keyhole.
- Horror line: a lantern with the flame upside down.

## 6 · Every post rule

Every post from any line carries:
1. One **Earth fact** most people never knew, with its source.
2. One **THYLORA fact**.
3. One piece of **math**, worked exactly.
4. Nothing hidden, nothing to trick anyone, nothing that asks for money without saying so.
