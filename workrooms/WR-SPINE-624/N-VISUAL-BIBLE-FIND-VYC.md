### 2.2 Must never be stylized away

1. The **trouser taper-to-ankle line** and the **loafer/sock break**. This is the one preserved, registered identity cue that survives at small sizes. Do not shorten, flare or cuff it unless the wardrobe record changes.
2. The **crease** on dress trousers, whenever the dress wardrobe state is in use.
3. **Measured proportions**, once measured. No heroic elongation, no chibi compression, no slimming.
4. **Stance and carriage** as they appear in R1. Do not swap in a generic "confident pose".
5. Do not invent **hat, accessory or hair outline** while those are UNKNOWN. An invented accessory becomes a false identifier.

### 2.3 Survival at small sizes

- **Minimum height rule:** a complete (full-figure) VYC silhouette must be at least **48 px tall** on the reference render, which has a short edge of 1080 px. In size-fraction terms, f = h_px / H_short ≥ 0.045.
- **Partial-presence minimum:** the smallest identifying fragment (the lower leg from knee to loafer) must be at least **24 px tall** on the reference render.
- **Thumbnail check:** downscale the render to a 320 px short edge. The figure must still read as a figure, not texture. If it does not, the image fails §6 fairness, not just this rule.
- **Simplification order** when space is tight: drop interior detail first, then fabric texture. Never drop the taper and loafer line, and never drop the head-to-shoulder step.

### 2.4 Contrast rules

- Measure contrast as a **CIELAB lightness difference, ΔL\***, between the silhouette's mean and a ring of background around it with width equal to 0.25 × h_px.
- **Hard floor ΔL\* ≥ 12**, so the figure is discoverable. **Soft ceiling ΔL\* ≤ 35**, so it does not dominate the image. The §6.2 formula tunes the value inside this band.
- **Colour-blind safety:** discoverability must come from lightness, not hue. The image must still pass the floor after a grayscale conversion and after deuteranopia, protanopia and tritanopia simulations.
- Never separate VYC from the background only by a red/green difference.

## 3 · Hidden placement, movement and posture

### 3.1 Hidden-placement rules

1. **Causal presence only** (`THY-LAW-LIFE-SNAPSHOT-001`). VYC appears only where the scene record states **time**, **place** and a **reason** for being there. If any one of the three is UNKNOWN, VYC is not placed.
2. **No story distortion.** Removing VYC from the frame must not change what the image says happened. VYC is never the cause of the depicted event unless the story record says so.
3. **Allowed hiding methods:** distance (a small figure in the background), partial occlusion by a scene object, reflection (glass or water) where the physics of the scene allow it, doorway or window framing, and crowd (only with non-identifiable bystanders).
4. **Forbidden methods:** camouflage that breaks physics (a figure merged into a texture), impossible scale, placement inside another character's face or body, text labels, hidden letters spelling "VYC", and anything requiring zoom beyond the fairness cap (§6.6).
5. **Occlusion cap:** at most 60% of the silhouette area may be occluded, and the §2.3 identifying fragment must remain visible.
6. **One VYC per image.** No decoys that look like VYC.

## 6 · FIND VYC recurring mechanic

### 6.1 Frequency

**Parameter:** N = the average number of published images per FIND VYC image, so the frequency is 1 in N.

**Proposal: N = 7**, as a `PRODUCTION_PLANNED` default.

Rationale:
- **Rarity keeps the search meaningful.** At 1 in 7, a viewer cannot assume VYC is in every image. Searching every image would dull both the story and the mechanic.
- **Trails fit inside a season.** A season of 28 published images yields 4 FIND VYC images, which is one complete 3- or 4-image trail (§6.5) per season.
- **Causal placement is scarce.** Under the Life Snapshot law, many scenes have no time/place/reason for VYC to be present. N = 7 leaves slack, so no placement ever has to be forced to hit a quota.
- **Selection is not calendar-slotted.** An image qualifies only if it passes the §7 preflight. If fewer qualify, the frequency drops. It never rises by relaxing causality.

**Acceptable range: 5 ≤ N ≤ 10.** Chairman decision to confirm.

### 6.2 Discoverability heuristic

**Class: HEURISTIC.** This is a production tuning aid, not a physical or perceptual law.

**D = C_n × A_n × T_n**

| Symbol | Name | Definition | Units | Domain |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| D | Discoverability score | Estimated readiness of the placement to be found | unitless | 0 to 1 |
| C_n | Normalized contrast | min(1, ΔL\* / ΔL\*_ref) | unitless | 0 to 1 |
| ΔL\* | Lightness difference | CIELAB L\* of the silhouette mean minus the background ring (§2.4), absolute value | L\* units (0–100 scale) | 0 to 100 |
| ΔL\*_ref | Reference contrast | 30 | L\* units | fixed |
| A_n | Normalized size | min(1, h_px / h_ref) | unitless | 0 to 1 |
| h_px | Silhouette height on the reference render | Measured in pixels at a 1080 px short edge | px | ≥ 0 |
| h_ref | Reference height | 72 px (1.5 × the 48 px minimum) | px | fixed |
| T_n | Dwell factor | 1 − e^(−t / τ) | unitless | 0 to 1 |
| t | Dwell time | Time a viewer spends actively searching the image | s | ≥ 0 |
| τ | Search time constant | 20 s | s | fixed |
| e | Euler's number | ≈ 2.71828 | unitless | constant |

**Thresholds.** The mechanic is "subtle but fairly discoverable after inspection".
- **Subtle:** D(t = 3 s) ≤ 0.25. A glance should not reveal VYC.
- **Fairly discoverable:** D(t = 60 s) ≥ 0.50. A minute of deliberate search should.
- **Hard floors:** ΔL\* ≥ 12 and h_px ≥ 48 (§2.3, §2.4) apply regardless of D.

**Assumptions.** The viewer sees the image at or near the reference size. Detection grows smoothly with search time. Contrast and size act multiplicatively, so either one near zero makes the figure effectively unfindable. Occlusion (§3.1, capped at 60%) and clutter are not in the formula; playtesting covers them.

**Failure condition.** The heuristic fails when playtest results diverge from it. If fewer than 40% of testers find VYC within 60 s while D(60) ≥ 0.5, or more than 25% find it within 3 s while D(3) ≤ 0.25, then τ, ΔL\*_ref and h_ref are re-fitted and the formula is re-registered.

**Plain-English meaning.** How findable VYC is depends on three things: how much he stands out in lightness, how big he is, and how long you look. We want almost nobody to spot him at a glance, and most careful lookers to find him within a minute.

**Child-readable meaning.** VYC is hiding. He should be hard to see if you only peek, but if you really look for a minute, you can find him.

**Worked example**
1. Placement: ΔL\* = 15, h_px = 72.
   - C_n = 15 / 30 = 0.50. A_n = 72 / 72 = 1.00.
   - At t = 60 s: T_n = 1 − e^(−3) = 1 − 0.0498 = 0.950, so D = 0.50 × 1.00 × 0.950 = **0.475**. That is below 0.50, so the placement **fails** "fairly discoverable".
2. Raise the contrast to ΔL\* = 18.
   - C_n = 0.60.
   - At t = 60 s: D = 0.60 × 1.00 × 0.950 = **0.570**, which passes (≥ 0.50).
   - At t = 3 s: T_n = 1 − e^(−0.15) = 1 − 0.861 = 0.139, so D = 0.60 × 0.139 = **0.084**, which passes (≤ 0.25).
   - The hard floors hold: 18 ≥ 12 and 72 ≥ 48. **The placement is accepted.**

### 6.5 Trails

- **Definition:** a trail is an ordered set of 3 or 4 FIND VYC images, (I₁, …, I_k). Each image contributes one clue, c_i, and the clues together compose **one world-continuity fact**, F, that no single image states.
- **Composability rule:** F must be derivable from c₁…c_k, and not derivable from any k − 1 of them. Every image matters.
- **Ordering:** images are released in trail order. A viewer who finds them out of order can still solve the trail once all k are found.
- **Existing records only:** F must be a fact already in the backend (or registered with the trail as `EDEREAIRAH_PROPOSED` and clearly labeled). A trail never announces a new canon fact by itself.

### 6.6 Fairness rules

1. **Zoom cap:** no clue may require zoom beyond **200%** of the reference display (1080 px short edge). At the cap, the identifying fragment must be at least 48 px on screen, matching the full-figure minimum.
2. **Colour-blind safe:** clues and the VYC figure pass the §2.4 lightness floor under grayscale and the three dichromacy simulations. No clue is carried by hue alone.
3. **No outside knowledge:** a clue must be solvable from published images and published world records, not from private or backend-only data.
4. **One answer:** each image has exactly one VYC location and one clue reading, as recorded in the answer key.
5. **No crop tricks:** VYC is inside the published frame of every distributed size (thumbnail, feed crop, full). If a platform crop would cut VYC, that size is excluded or the placement moves.
6. **Accessibility:** every FIND VYC image ships alt text that does **not** reveal VYC. A separate, opt-in, audio-described hint path is provided so blind or low-vision participants can take part. Its design is a queued item (§8).
7. **No private data:** no clue exposes a real private person, a child, or medical detail.

- **Commit** = SHA-256(canonical_json(record)), published with the image.
- **Chain hash** = SHA-256(prev_hash ‖ commit). It becomes the next record's `prev_hash`, so any edit to an earlier record breaks every later link.
- **Salt** prevents brute-forcing the bbox or clue from the public commit.
- **Reveal:** at season close, publish the full records. Anyone can recompute the commits and the chain.
- **Custody:** records are held privately by the production director until reveal. They are never stored in the public repo before reveal.
