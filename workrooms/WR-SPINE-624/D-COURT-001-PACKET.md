### 4.4 A comparable problem from our world (EDEREAIRAH_PROPOSED story)
**Author:** Tamsin Rourke (analyst), reviewed by Judge Oduya.
**"The River Court Filing Drift."** On the Tenancy & Small-Claims bench, the Filing Hall disposed of fewer cases than it received for four straight quarters (quarter = 90 Earth-read days). About one hearing in four was continued because the file was not ready; about one person in five did not appear. Backlog grew every quarter. No one person was failing — the steps did not check each other.

### 4.5 What we changed (EDEREAIRAH_PROPOSED)
**Authors:** Petrakis (1–2), Aven (3–4), Varga (5).
1. **Completeness check at intake** — one-page checklist; incomplete files returned same-day with a list, not scheduled.
2. **Ready-to-hear gate** — no hearing slot until intake = COMPLETE.
3. **Plain-language notice** — date, time, place, and consequence of missing it printed largest (echoes Earth summons redesign, S4 — a parallel, not evidence for the world).
4. **Reminder + follow-through** — reminder before hearing; "what you must do next" line on every order; access desk follow-through.
5. **Weekly clearance ledger** — clearance rate, backlog, age, FTA, ready-to-hear share on one sheet.

## 5 · Math (Math Display Law — every formula)

**Author:** Tamsin Rourke (analyst); readback checked by Hollis Varga. None of these is a PHYSICAL_LAW. Shared-vision equation `MATH-SHARED-VISION-622` (Vc = S × I × G × C) is referenced for the desk's convergence scoring only and **not redefined** here.

### M1 · Clearance rate
- **Formula:** CR_t = D_t ÷ F_t
- **Readback:** "C-R sub t equals D sub t divided by F sub t."
- **Symbols:** CR_t clearance rate in period t; D_t dispositions (outgoing cases) in t; F_t filings incl. reopened/reactivated (incoming) in t. Subscript t = period index (month or 90-day quarter).
- **Units:** D, F = cases (count); CR unitless (ratio; ×100 for %).
- **Domain:** D_t ≥ 0, F_t > 0. **Scale:** typically 0.5–1.5. **Threshold:** ≥ 1.00 (NCSC aspiration, S1).
- **Assumptions:** same case-type scope and counting rules for D and F; reopens counted in F.
- **Failure condition:** F_t = 0 (undefined); inconsistent counting; a period with a one-time purge of dead cases inflates D.
- **Plain meaning:** did the court finish at least as many cases as came in?
- **Child meaning:** if 10 new toys land in the box and you put away 8, the box gets fuller.
- **Worked example (Earth, S1):** 913 ÷ 1,083 = 0.84 → 84%. **World (SIMULATION):** 1,020 ÷ 1,200 = 0.85; after 1,380 ÷ 1,200 = 1.15.
- **Class:** FORMAL_SYSTEM_LAW (definition); the ≥100% target is HEURISTIC.

### M2 · Backlog stock-flow
- **Formula:** B_{t+1} = B_t + F_t − D_t
- **Readback:** "B sub t-plus-one equals B sub t plus F sub t minus D sub t."
- **Symbols:** B_t pending cases at start of t; F_t, D_t as M1.
- **Units:** cases (count). **Domain:** all ≥ 0. **Scale:** hundreds to millions.
- **Threshold:** B trending flat or down across ≥ 3 periods.
- **Assumptions:** closed system — every case enters by F and leaves by D; transfers counted.
- **Failure condition:** uncounted transfers, inactive-status cases moved off the count, data corrections.
- **Plain meaning:** the pile at the end is the pile at the start plus what came in minus what went out.
- **Child meaning:** bucket water = old water + poured in − poured out.
- **Worked example (World SIMULATION):** 2,400 + 1,200 − 1,020 = 2,580; after 4 quarters 3,120. After change: 3,120 + 1,200 − 1,380 = 2,940 … → 2,400.
- **Class:** FORMAL_SYSTEM_LAW (conservation/accounting identity for counts).

### M3 · Failure-to-appear rate and reminder effect
- **Formulas:** FTA_t = M_t ÷ S_t ; FTA_after = FTA_before × (1 − r)
- **Readback:** "F-T-A sub t equals M sub t over S sub t; F-T-A after equals F-T-A before times one minus r."
- **Symbols:** M_t hearings where a required party did not appear; S_t hearings scheduled with a required appearance; r relative reduction from an intervention.
- **Units:** M, S = hearings (count); FTA, r unitless.
- **Domain:** 0 ≤ M ≤ S, S > 0; 0 ≤ r < 1. **Scale:** FTA 0–0.5 typical. **Threshold (pilot):** r ≥ 0.10 in the reminder-eligible docket.
- **Assumptions:** same docket types before/after; r is transferable only as an estimate — Earth evidence is 0.13–0.21 in NYC low-level criminal summonses (S4), not civil tenancy.
- **Failure condition:** docket mix changes; "appear" defined differently (remote vs. in person); reminder reaches few people.
- **Plain meaning:** share of people who didn't come; reminders cut that share by some fraction.
- **Child meaning:** if 5 friends are invited and 1 forgets, a reminder note might mean nobody forgets.
- **Worked example (World SIMULATION):** 330 ÷ 1,500 = 0.22; 0.22 × (1 − 0.20) = 0.176 → 264 of 1,500.
- **Class:** FTA definition = FORMAL_SYSTEM_LAW; the reduction r = EMPIRICAL_MODEL.

### M4 · Time-to-disposition percentile and standard compliance
- **Formulas:** P_q = q-th percentile of {T_i}, T_i = date_disposed,i − date_filed,i ; S(τ) = count(T_i ≤ τ) ÷ N
- **Readback:** "P sub q is the q-th percentile of T sub i; S of tau equals the count of T sub i at most tau, over N."
- **Symbols:** T_i days case i was open; q percentile (50 = median, 90); N cases disposed in period; τ standard window (days).
- **Units:** T, P_q, τ = Earth-read days (Earth: calendar days); S unitless.
- **Domain:** T_i ≥ 0. **Threshold (Earth misdemeanor, S3):** S(60) ≥ 0.75, S(90) ≥ 0.90, S(180) ≥ 0.98.
- **Assumptions:** CourTools excludes periods of inactivity beyond court control (S2); same exclusions before/after.
- **Failure condition:** measuring only disposed cases hides old pending cases — must be read with age of pending (M5).
- **Plain meaning:** how long cases take, for the typical case and the slow tail.
- **Child meaning:** line up everyone by how long they waited; the middle person is the median.
- **Worked example (World SIMULATION):** median 140 → 120 days; P90 410 → 340 days. (Earth check: if 870 of 1,000 misdemeanors close within 90 days, S(90) = 0.87 < 0.90 → below standard. Illustrative numbers, not Earth data.)
- **Class:** FORMAL_SYSTEM_LAW (definition); the standards are HEURISTIC benchmarks.

### M5 · Little's law (mean time in system)
- **Formula:** W = L ÷ λ
- **Readback:** "W equals L divided by lambda."
- **Symbols:** W mean time a case spends pending; L mean number pending; λ arrival rate (filings per day).
- **Units:** W days; L cases; λ cases/day.
- **Domain:** L ≥ 0, λ > 0. **Scale:** W 30–1,000 days.
- **Assumptions:** long-run steady state (arrivals ≈ departures). **Failure condition:** backlog growing or shrinking fast — result is only an estimate.
- **Plain meaning:** a big pile with slow arrivals means each case waits long.
- **Child meaning:** 20 kids in line, 2 new ones arrive each minute → each waits about 10 minutes.
- **Worked example (World SIMULATION):** 2,400 ÷ (1,200 ÷ 90 = 13.33/day) = 180 days.
- **Class:** FORMAL_SYSTEM_LAW (queueing theorem under stated assumptions).

### M6 · Clerk capacity
- **Formula:** D_max = (H × u) ÷ h̄
- **Readback:** "D max equals H times u, divided by h-bar."
- **Symbols:** H clerk hours available in period; u share of hours on case work; h̄ mean clerk hours per disposition.
- **Units:** H, h̄ hours; u unitless; D_max cases.
- **Domain:** H > 0; 0 < u ≤ 1; h̄ > 0. **Threshold:** D_max ≥ F_t (otherwise CR < 1 is structural).
- **Assumptions:** h̄ stable; clerk time is the binding constraint (not judge time).
- **Failure condition:** bottleneck is elsewhere (judge hours, rooms); vacancies change H mid-period.
- **Plain meaning:** hours available ÷ hours per case = most cases we can finish.
- **Child meaning:** if each puzzle takes 2 hours and you have 10 hours, you finish 5 puzzles.
- **Worked example (World SIMULATION):** 6 clerks × 450 h = 2,700 h; × 0.8 = 2,160 h; ÷ 2.1 = 1,029 (before); ÷ 1.55 = 1,394 (after).
- **Class:** EMPIRICAL_MODEL (workload model).

### M7 · Wasted-hearing load
- **Formula:** X = S × p_c
- **Readback:** "X equals S times p sub c."
- **Symbols:** X hearings consumed without progress; S hearings scheduled; p_c share continued because the file was not ready.
- **Units:** X, S hearings; p_c unitless. **Domain:** 0 ≤ p_c ≤ 1. **Threshold (pilot):** ready-to-hear share (1 − p_c) ≥ 0.85.
- **Assumptions:** "not ready" reason is coded at the hearing. **Failure condition:** reasons not coded or coded inconsistently.
- **Plain meaning:** how many hearing slots are spent going nowhere.
- **Child meaning:** showing up to a game with no ball wastes the field.
- **Worked example (World SIMULATION):** 1,500 × 0.27 = 405 → 1,500 × 0.12 = 180; 225 slots recovered.
- **Class:** EMPIRICAL_MODEL.

### M8 · Pilot decision rule
- **Formula:** Δ = (x_after − x_base) ÷ x_base ; PASS if Δ meets threshold for ≥ 2 of 3 pilot months
- **Readback:** "Delta equals x after minus x base, over x base."
- **Units:** unitless. **Domain:** x_base ≠ 0. **Failure condition:** seasonal swing larger than effect; baseline too short.
- **Plain meaning:** did it move enough, often enough?
- **Child meaning:** did your score go up in at least two of three games?
- **Worked example:** FTA 0.22 → 0.19: Δ = −0.136 → meets r ≥ 0.10.
- **Class:** HEURISTIC.
