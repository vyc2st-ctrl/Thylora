## THE POST

**HOOK**
One herb. How big is the world around it?

**MAIN QUESTION**
How many people, roads, records and decisions stand behind one bundle of leaves on a kitchen table?

**WHAT WE SEE**
A royal cook at first light. A cut bundle set down beside the day list. A knife, a jar, a pot off the heat, a small scale. One room.

**WHAT WE KNOW** *(backend records, not description)*
- The kitchen runs on five shift segments. The fire is never unattended while alight; at night one hand watches it banked.
- The herb ground is a short same-day walk from the kitchen. No gate is crossed, so no security check applies.
- The Royal Cook — not the Provisioner — controls what is cut from it each day.
- Bought goods arrive by cart on the approach road, are met at the gate by the Provisioner with the Captain present, and only become stores after that crossing.
- Six stores hold what arrives: pantry, dry store, cold store, meat larder, herb store, cellar. The herb store is the one the Cook keeps herself.
- Seven waste routes run the other way. Kitchen compost goes back to the herb ground it came from.

**WHAT IS INFERENCE**
- That she cut it herself this morning follows from her holding the daily cut, but no cutting record exists — whether the daily cut is written down at all is unruled.
- That the bundle reached the table without crossing the gate follows from the herb ground's position, not from a movement record.

**WHAT IS UNKNOWN**
- The castle's name, city and territory.
- The plant's own name in its own world.
- Who made the knife, the jar, the pot, the scale or the table. Nine cookware classes exist. Eleven maker slots exist. Every one is empty.
- How many hands the scullery keeps.

**THE CHAIN**
HERB → GROWER → LAND → HARVEST → TRANSPORT → RECEIVING → STORAGE → COOK → RECIPE → HISTORICAL USE → EVIDENCE → MODERN COMPARISON → UNKNOWN
Eight of those steps carry a record. Two are stated inference. Three are blank, and stay blank.

**CASTLE CONNECTION**
One bundle touches the garden, the road, the gate, the guard, the stores, the accounts, the fire, the waste route and the compost that feeds the garden again. The chain closes. That is what makes it a world and not a set.

**HISTORICAL EVIDENCE** *(Earth side only — five layers, never merged)*
- HISTORICAL USE — DOCUMENTED. Long culinary and domestic use around the Mediterranean; strewing and preserving; hair and scalp washes; steams and infusions for headache and digestion; carried at funerals and weddings as a token of remembrance.

- LATER / MODERN EVIDENCE — LIMITED, NOT ESTABLISHED. Small trials on rosemary oil in hair loss and on rosemary aroma and cognition; small samples, varied methods, inconsistent results.
