## 1 · HEAD

Tomorrow's post is not an infographic. It is a **window into EdereAirah** — one
fixed frame of the working Royal Kitchen in the Upper Court, held long enough
that the room's own economy is legible: who owns the knife, who grew the herbs,

## 4 · EXACT SCENE

**Place:** Royal Kitchen, service range, north side of the Upper Court,
EdereAirah Castle. Working room, not a display kitchen.
**Time:** late morning, third week of a cold season. Cook's shift mid-service.
**Duration of the moment:** ~1.5 seconds.

The room is a long vaulted hall, wider than deep, hearth wall on the outer
(north) face, high window band above the hearth on the same wall, service door
on the short east wall giving onto the garden stair.

Reading the frame left to right:

1. **Left third** — the live hearth. Iron crane swung half out, one pot on it.
   Banked second hearth beside it, cold, raked. Ash bucket under the raking
   ledge. Smoke column rising and then bending right, toward the room, because
   the east door is open.
2. **Centre** — the long worktable, scrubbed elm, running away from camera.
   **Inés Morales** stands on its far side, hands on the board, her
   brown-handled knife down through a parsnip and not through it. A shallow
   copper tray of cut root to her right, the uncut remainder to her left. She
   has stopped. Her head has come up and turned about 30° to her right, toward
   the door. Her hands have not moved.
3. **Centre-right, second plane** — **Clara Bennett** at the hearth end of the
   table, side-on, both hands on a stoneware bowl she is tipping toward the pot
   Inés cannot reach. She is mid-task and has not stopped. She has not looked up.
4. **Right third, third plane** — **Veronica Hall** two steps inside the open
   east door, stopped because the room is busy and she is waiting for a gap at
   the table. A shallow willow basket of cut herbs on her left forearm. Her
   right hand is on the door's inner post, still, having just pushed it. She is
   looking at the table surface — for the clear space, not at Inés.

**What the frame does not contain:** no eye contact, no matched framing, no
symmetry between Inés and Veronica, no light that touches only one of them, no
glow, no dust shaft, no hand reaching, no object passing between them, no
onlooker, no mirror, no portrait, no crest, no open locket, no legible
inscription on any personal object.

**Inés's state, exactly:** she feels comfort, concern, protectiveness and
familiarity arrive at once and out of proportion to a kitchen girl at a door.
She does not know why. She attributes it to nothing. She does not know Veronica
is related to her and nothing in the frame, the captions or the alt text permits
that inference. She will go back to the parsnip.

**Grammar:** antique etched historical-world structure + controlled living
colour. Base is a sepia-iron etched line — warm-grey line on bone paper, cross-
hatched, plate-textured, with the etching's own tonal range doing all structural
work. Colour is then admitted only where the world is alive.

**Four colour zones. No fifth.**

## 9 · EMOTIONAL BEATS

Four beats inside ~1.5 seconds. All three read as motor events, not expressions.

**Beat 1 — 0.0s · The room does not change.**
Pot at a low roll. Clara tipping. Inés cutting. Draft already moving the smoke.
The frame's baseline is *work in progress*, established before anyone arrives.

**Beat 2 — 0.4s · Interruption at the hand.**
The knife stops. Not lifted — **stopped**, down through the parsnip and not
through it. The stroke is incomplete. This is the whole emotional event and it
is located in a wrist.

**Beat 3 — 0.9s · The head follows the hand.**
Inés's head comes up and turns 30°. Her jaw is not slack, her brows are not
raised, her mouth is closed. The only facial fact is that her attention has
left the board. Face is 3/4 away from camera and under-described by design.

**Beat 4 — 1.4s · Nothing is resolved.**
Veronica has not noticed. Clara has not looked up. The basket has not been set
down. Inés does not speak, step, reach, or soften. The comfort, concern,
protectiveness and familiarity all arrive and are given nowhere to go.

**What is not in the beats:** recognition, realisation, dawning, softening,
tears, a hand to the chest, a touched pendant, a held breath rendered as a
parted mouth, a second glance, any gesture toward the locket, any beat that
resolves the feeling into a cause.
