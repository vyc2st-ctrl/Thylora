## Every formula this packet uses, identified

None of these appear in the email itself (it stays plain for a school reader). They power the family tools in items 2 and 3, and they appear inside those tools with their names attached.

| Where it is used | Expression | Name / registry ID | Type of math | What each letter means |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Water-bill family tool | C = r × t × p | Water Cost (**MATH-WATER-COST-657**, PROPOSED) | Rate × time × price (multiplication of a rate) | C = cost, r = flow in gallons per minute, t = minutes running, p = price per gallon |
| Family-record tool | K = W × Y × P × A | Sayreth (**MATH-SAYRETH-646**, PROPOSED) | Product of factors; any 0 → 0 | W = who wrote it, Y = year/when, P = place they stood, A = another record agrees (each 0–3) |
| Find the Gap projects | G = P × M × E × D | Goal Equation (**MATH-GPMED-639**, PROPOSED; letters awaiting Chairman confirmation) | Product of factors | P = purpose, M = method, E = evidence, D = discipline (proposed reading) |

**Why multiplication everywhere (our logic):** a zero in any one part makes the whole result zero. This is the same rule as R = O × T × B × C. A record with no known writer can't be trusted however old it is, and a plan with no evidence goes nowhere however good the purpose is.
