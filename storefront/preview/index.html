The equation this is built against

S_store = I × C × D × T
S
S_store — storefront experience strength. Multiplicative, so any factor at zero makes the whole store zero. That is the point: a beautiful store nobody understands is worth nothing, and a clear store with nothing in it is worth nothing.
I
Identity — unmistakably ErsatzReality. Could this page belong to any other store? Today: the nav is Home / Catalog / Contact, Shopify's factory default.
C
Clarity — the visitor understands what they can do here within one screen. Today there is no page anywhere on the store that says what ErsatzReality is.
D
Depth — every item connects outward to a world, a story or an open question. Today: zero metafields and zero metaobjects exist, so nothing can connect to anything.
T
Trust — polished, coherent, usable, professional. Today: four of eight collections render completely empty, and “New This Week” promises work that is done while showing one item.

Measured honestly against the live store on 2026-09-18, D is at or near zero and C is at or near zero. Under a multiplicative rule that forces a HOLD on the experience as a whole — which is the correct reading, and the reason this preview exists instead of a theme edit.
