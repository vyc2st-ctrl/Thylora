Page 1

BRAMBLE WICK — The Lantern That Wouldn't Go Out. A nighttime story from EdereAriah.

In the hill-town of Bramble Wick, every house kept one small lantern in the window after sunset. Nobody was required to. It was simply what people did. The lanterns told travelers: Someone lives here. Someone is awake. If you need help, knock.

Page 2 — 1. The Wind / 2. The Knock

Mara Vale was nine and had been trusted with the east-window lantern for exactly six nights. On the seventh night, a storm rolled down from the black hills… "You can put it out," her older brother said. "Nobody is traveling in this." Mara looked through the wet glass. "That is exactly when somebody might need it."

Across the valley, one light after another disappeared behind the rain. Mara's stayed. Near midnight came three knocks… At the door stood an old courier with mud to his knees and a broken wheel-pin in his hand. "I did not know whose house it was. I only knew somebody had left a way to be found."

Page 3 — 3. What the Lantern Was For

"Keep it," he told Mara. "People remember lamps. But sometimes they forget what the lamp actually did." … Because she understood the question underneath it: When somebody cannot see a way forward, what can we leave visible enough for them to find us?
