Who made it, and what we are to it

A warehouse that will one day hold other people's work has to answer this on every single item, before you pay. There are six states and no others. Everything on the floor today is state one.

ErsatzV owned
Ours. We made it and we answer for it. All fourteen items on the floor today.
Authorized Earth Maker
Made on Earth under a written agreement, as an Authorized Earth Maker of an EdereAirah ErsatzV Brand Ware. The design is ours, the making is theirs, and the agreement is real and named. Nobody holds this.
Selected seller
Someone else's product, let in deliberately after review. Their product, their responsibility, sold here. Nobody holds this.
Sponsored
Money moved to put it in front of you. Labelled on the item itself, never buried in a footer. Nothing is sponsored.
Supported
We give a maker time, tools or distribution without owning or endorsing what they make. Nobody holds this.
Listed, no endorsement
Present in the catalog and nothing more. Nothing is listed on these terms.

Listed is not sponsored. Sold is not endorsed. A maker is not an Authorized Earth Maker. Those are three separate states with three separate records, and the item tells you which one it holds. Treating them as interchangeable is how a marketplace ends up lying to the person paying.

What a VLEGH is, since it will be asked

Not a trading card

A VLEGH is an EdereAirah object. Earth trading cards are a useful reference for one narrow thing — how people collect, trade and care for small objects — and for nothing else. A VLEGH is not a rarity tier with a picture on it.

Every VLEGH carries:

  • its provenance, and its identity within the world
  • where it came from, and where in EdereAirah that place is
  • who created it, which edition it belongs to, and who made this particular one
  • its story, and its relationships to other objects, houses and people
  • its authenticity, held as a record that can be read back rather than asserted on the object
  • a deeper digital record that outlives the physical piece
  • and a physical geometry, material and visual language that is its own — not a rectangle with a border

None of that is manufactured. The specification is the honest state of it today, and a specification is not an object. When a VLEGH can be held, it will be behind door four, with the name of the maker on it.

What we do with what you send us

These are not preferences. They are the gates.

Private by default. Anything you send is used to make your order and nothing else. It is never published, never shown as an example, never used to train anything, and never shown to anyone outside the work.

Publication consent is asked every time and the default answer is no. Saying no costs nothing and changes nothing about what you receive. For anything that may contain a child or a pregnancy, there is no yes to give at all — the option does not exist, deliberately, and the system refuses to record one.

Where a child appears, a guardian has to say so themselves. Not a tickbox on someone else's behalf.

Your material stays yours. Your photographs, your words and your likeness. We claim none of them and will license none of them to anyone.

Deletion is real. Ask, and the files are removed from storage. Not flagged, not hidden, not soft-deleted with the bytes still sitting there. The system will not report a deletion as complete unless the storage is verified empty.

No public route exists for customer imagery. There is no gallery, no showcase and no feed to end up in, because none was built. That is the strongest version of this promise available: not a policy against publishing, an absence of anywhere to publish to.
