## 1. What the product is

One entry = one **land**, one **people**, one **period**, one **named written source**, and the
uses that source actually attributes. Sold as a pair:

| Artifact | Audience | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Adult reference sheet** | Adult reader | Names the source, the date, the holding institution, the plant, the attributed historical use, what modern pharmacology did or did not carry forward, and the safety position. |
| **EdereAriah mirror companion** | Family / younger reader, read alongside an adult | Tells the same entry as a story about people and place. Carries the wonder and the history. Carries **no** preparation, no dose, no instruction to use anything. |

## 2. The organizing rule (non-negotiable)

**Organize by land / people / period. Never flatten a people into one practice.**

"African herbal medicine" is not an entry. "Egyptian medicine" is not an entry.
An entry is `Nile Valley (Kemet) — New Kingdom, c. 1550 BCE — Ebers Papyrus`.

A continent is not a practice. A people is not a period. A period is not a document.
Two entries may sit on the same land and still be different entries, because a
different people, a different century, or a different source makes them different
knowledge. Where the same plant appears on several lands, each land gets its own
entry and its own source — the plant does not become the unit, the record does.

## 3. The source rule

Every attributed use carries, on the sheet itself:

- **Source** — the named document or compilation
- **Date of the source** — and, separately, the era of the practice it records
- **Where it is held or published** — institution, edition, or publication
- **What the source actually says** — the attributed use, not a modern gloss on it
- **Confidence** — `ATTESTED` (the named source states it) / `REPORTED` (a later
  compiler records it) / `CONTESTED` (sources disagree) / `UNVERIFIED` (not yet confirmed)

**A record's date is not the practice's age.** Much of what was written down about
African, Indigenous American, and Pacific plant knowledge was first recorded by
colonial-era compilers, centuries or millennia after the practice began, by people
outside it, sometimes hostile to it. Where that is the case the sheet says so on its
face. The compiler's date goes in the source line; the practice's own antiquity, and
the fact that the written record starts late because of who was doing the writing,
goes in the provenance note. Reporting a 1937 colonial compilation as if it were the
origin of the knowledge would be the same flattening this series exists to refuse.

## 4. Safety gate (blocking — no entry publishes without it)

This is a **history product, not medical advice.** The gate:

1. Every plant carries a **toxicity line**. Where the historical preparation is
   dangerous, or where the raw plant is dangerous and only processing makes it safe,
   the sheet says so plainly and specifically.
2. The adult sheet carries **no doses and no preparation methods** that a reader could
   follow. It reports what a source recorded; it does not hand over a recipe.
3. The mirror companion carries **no preparation, no dose, no plant identification for
   collection, and no instruction to use anything.** It is a story about people.
4. Every entry where the plant is toxic, easily confused with a toxic look-alike, or
   interacts with common medication carries a **named hazard**, not a general caution.
5. Reviewed and recorded against `thylora_conveyor_safety_review` before
   `safety_reviewed` is allowed to pass.

## 5. Completion definition

An entry is complete when all of the following are true:

- [ ] Land / people / period named and distinct from every other entry
- [ ] Named source with date, holding institution, and confidence per claim
- [ ] Attributed historical use stated in the source's own terms
- [ ] Modern pharmacological outcome stated, including where it went nowhere
- [ ] Toxicity and hazard lines present for every named plant
- [ ] Provenance note where the written record post-dates the practice
- [ ] Adult sheet final
- [ ] EdereAriah mirror companion final, and carries no instruction to use
- [ ] Safety review recorded
- [ ] Both artifacts hashed and staged for protected delivery
