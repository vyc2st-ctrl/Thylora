## WHY A PERSON WOULD BUY IT

The free post gives them one good story. The file gives them something they cannot easily
get: **a plant history that tells them which parts of itself to distrust.** Almost everything
written about herbs collapses folklore, marketing and pharmacology into one confident voice.
This file keeps them in four separate registers and puts its own unknowns in print, at the
same size as its claims. The reader is buying a method they can carry to the next plant they
read about — and at $5.00 they are buying it for less than the coffee they read it over.

The secondary reason is that it is a **collectible numbered object**, not a PDF handout.
File 001 exists to be followed by File 002.

## RELATION TO FUTURE SHELF ITEMS

File 001 fixes the spine every later file inherits: the four registers, the identity/serial
page, the declared-unknowns page, the plate pair (specimen + mechanism), and the numbering.
Later files change the plant, not the architecture. That is what makes the collection a
series rather than a folder, and it is what lets the six-file bound collection exist later
without a redesign.
