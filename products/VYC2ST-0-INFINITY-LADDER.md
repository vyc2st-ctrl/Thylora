### Cross-product observations
- **The micro-price problem.** At $0.99 and $1.49 the processor's fixed fee takes a large share of each sale (see `PRICING-PROPOSED.json`). A **library or bundle** (for example Detective, Teacher Pack and Build Your Gate for one price) or the future **prepaid credit** model fixes this better than lone card sales. The credit model involves stored value and minors, so it is ESCALATE.
- **One engine, many skins.** Case File (10 cards, second-gear questions) and Gate (question, proof, who decides, double-check, neighbours) are both **formats**. Formats are what license and localise.
- **Children appear in four of five lanes.** The consistent answer is: **no child accounts; a guardian or teacher holds access; no data collected from children.** This stays in place until LEGAL review says otherwise.
