The fixed barrier

This block is appended verbatim to every image prompt below, and to every THYLORA image from here forward. It does not need to be requested again.

One correction to the brief. The instruction named the rule as “no swirls, no smoke-like haze, no rainbow interference.” That is the prohibition half only. The locked backend rule also carries a required-form half and a numeric intensity band — an image that merely avoids swirls still fails the gate if the barrier is absent, drifting, or outside 0.18–0.28. Both halves are applied below.

Model:      I_seen(x,y,t) = T(x,y) * W(x,y,t) + B(x,y)
Barrier:    B = g1*F + g2*S + g3*E + g4*D      ordering  g1 > g2 > g3 > g4
Stationary: dB(x,y)/dt = 0        World:  dW(x,y,t)/dt may be nonzero
Nulled:     swirl_term = 0        smoke_like_term = 0
Intensity:  0.18 – 0.28 relative visual weight — subtle but unmistakable
Priority:   SUBJECT > WORLD > DEPTH > BARRIER
Geometry:   EARTH VIEWER → STATIONARY VIEWING FILM → THYLORA/EDEREARIAH WORLD

Required — the barrier must be present

  • F — faint material texture of a fixed pane
  • S — restrained scratches / projector gate wear
  • E — fixed edge and frame behaviour at the border
  • D — slight density variation across the pane

Prohibited

  • swirls · rainbow rings · rainbow interference
  • smoke-like haze · atmospheric drift · uniform blur
  • portal · bubble · force field · glowing edge · touch ripple
  • lens-flare dominance · sparkle · decorative interference
  • barrier that tracks the subject or moves with the camera
  • barrier treated as an object inside the scene

People and objects inside the scene do not see, touch, investigate or react to the viewing film. It is viewer-side only. Palette across all three: restrained sepia, charcoal, umber and cream, with one selective low-saturation accent per image and no other saturated colour in frame.
