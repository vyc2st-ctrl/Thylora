One field, complete

I · Inspect

Known, unknown, next step.

Three lists. What you can evidence. What you cannot. The one thing you do next.

This is the field people fake, by moving items from "unknown" to "known" on the strength of a strong feeling. The discipline is simple and hard: an item is known only if you can point at where it is written. Everything else is unknown — including things you are confident about.

A long unknown list is not a failure. An empty unknown list on a decision of any size is almost always a lie you are telling yourself.

Done when: every "known" has a pointer, and the next step is one action, not a plan.

The scoring rule, complete

QYRIS does not average. A check is PASS only when all five fields are answered. Any field blank is HOLD, and hold means the work does not move to active, ready, or done — regardless of how far along it already is.

And the row that matters most: work already in flight with no check at all is a hold, not a pass. Otherwise the gate only ever catches the careful.
