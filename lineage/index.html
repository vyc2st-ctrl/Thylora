ROOF · CANADA DESK

“My great-grandmother came out of Canada.”

Graded FAMILY_TOLD, word for word: she came out of Canada, before slavery, and was never enslaved. No story is assumed. Every origin hypothesis starts equal, and only records and DNA move it (gate MATH-LINEAGE-HYPOTHESIS-GATE-656).

How the gate works

  • "Enslaved" is held at zero by the family's word; only a primary record naming her could reopen it.
  • Statements like "enslaved", "freedom seeker", "Great Migration" — or "was Indigenous" — are blocked until two independent sources and a direct record prove them.
  • Her race is read in every record, year by year. A change such as Indian → colored is recorded as a finding of erased heritage, never dismissed as noise.
  • Full case: Ma Sweet — the open case.
