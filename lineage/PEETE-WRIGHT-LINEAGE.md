# Peete · Wright Lineage — Record of Evidence

**Owner:** VYC (Chairman), son of Walter James Peete and Eddie Ree Wright Peete
**Opened:** 2026-10-03
**Rule:** Every fact below carries its source and a status. Nothing is promoted
from LEAD to CONFIRMED without a record that says it.

Status key:
- **CONFIRMED** — stated directly by a record (census, Social Security file, marriage record, obituary).
- **STRONG LEAD** — consistent across sources but no single record yet ties it.
- **LEAD** — a direction worth checking. Not a finding.
- **REJECTED** — checked and does not belong to this family.

### Generation 3 · James "Jim" Peete & Birdy Hall — CONFIRMED
- **James Peete:** born about **1866**, Tennessee; died **19 August 1928**, Nashville, Davidson County, age 63.
- 1870: Haywood County, Tennessee, in his parents' household.
- 1880: Civil District 8, Humphreys County, Tennessee.

- Sources: FamilySearch G8BW-Q4Q; 1870 & 1880 U.S. Census; Tennessee Deaths 1914–1966

### Generation 4 · Richard Peete & Susan — CONFIRMED (in 1870)
- **Richard Peete:** born about **1822**. **Susan:** born about **1830**.
- 1870 U.S. Census: Haywood County, Tennessee, with children **Hester Peete** (b. ~1862)
  and **James Peete** (b. ~1866).
- **Reading:** a Black man born in West Tennessee or Virginia in 1822 was born enslaved.
  1870 is the first census that recorded Richard and Susan by name. Before it they
  appear only as unnamed marks in the 1850/1860 slave schedules, under the enslaver's name.
- Source: FamilySearch G8BW-Q4Q (parents' panel, 1870 census attachment).

### Generation 5+ · Before 1865 — STRONG LEAD
- A white Peete family moved from **Mecklenburg County, Virginia** to Tipton County,
  Tennessee: John Peete (b. 1810 Mecklenburg VA, d. ~1892 Tipton Co.; father Edwin
  Hart Peete) and John Speed Peete (1818–1878). The Library of Virginia holds
  Peete and Peete-Speed family Bible records for Mecklenburg County.
- A Peete/Grandberry family reunion post (Mason, TN) states members of "the Peete
  family of slaveowners who brought the Peetes from Mecklenburg Va." are buried at Mason.
- **What proves or kills it:** wills, estate inventories and slave schedules (1850,
  1860) for the white Peetes in Tipton and Haywood counties. Look for an enslaved
  male born ~1822 (Richard / Dick) and a female born ~1830 (Susan / Sukey).

### Related Peete branch — LEAD
- **Orange Peete & Mariah (Moriah) Lowe**, Stanton / Phelan area, Tipton County.
  Orange died 1918 ("a well known and respected colored man… buried in the
  vicinity of Stanton" — *The Covington Leader*). Son Clarence Lelan Peete b. 1894.
- Descendants run the Facebook group "Peete's family worldwide," where Hattie
  Peete Jones (daughter of Walter Peete and Eddie Ree Wright) has posted.
- **Not yet tied** to Richard Peete. Orange and Richard may be brothers, cousins,
  or held by the same enslaver. The 1870 Haywood/Tipton census pages decide it.

## 3 · The shape of it (one line)

**Mecklenburg Co., Virginia (enslaved, lead) → Haywood & Tipton Co., Tennessee
(Richard & Susan, 1870) → Mason, TN (Walter, 1936) → St. Louis (1950s–1990)**
meets
**Panola & Quitman Co., Mississippi (Eugene & Hester Wright) → St. Louis & Memphis.**

Both sides came up out of the cotton counties into St. Louis in the Great Migration.
