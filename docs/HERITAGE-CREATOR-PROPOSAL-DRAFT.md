## What we saw

He reads primary documents on camera, like the 1890 *Extra Census Bulletin: The Five
Civilized Tribes in Indian Territory*, and connects them to Black and Native family history.
He finds the records fast. The weak spots are structure and presentation: the points are
strong but they spread out, and viewers can lose the thread or the citation.

## What THYLORA offers

| Offer | What he gets | What it costs him |
|---|---|---|
| **Records desk** | Our team pulls and transcribes the documents he names (Dawes cards, Freedmen rolls, Guion Miller files, census pages) with full citations | Nothing |
| **Clean edit pass** | Chapter markers, on-screen document callouts, a one-line thesis at the top, a source card at the end of each video | Nothing for a pilot of 3 videos |
| **EDF editions** | Each strong video becomes an Ersatz Digital Format product: **Read** (an illustrated report), **Hear** (narrated), **Watch** (the video), **Explore** (the actual documents, maps and sources) | Revenue share, below |
| **Store products** | Annotated document packs (for example, "Reading a Dawes Freedmen Card"), family research workbooks, course bundles in the THYLORA store | Revenue share, below |
| **RAE Link channel** | A channel on THYLORA's own media network with a published, itemized earnings statement (gross, fees, platform share, creator share, payment date and evidence) | Revenue share, below |
| **Shows and film** | Development of a documentary or series from his strongest threads, through THYLORA's Story Studio pipeline (Story → Writers → Rights → Casting → Previs → Review → Release) | Separate deal; his name and credit stay with him |

**Revenue share:** to be set by the Chairman. The RAE Link ledger already supports a
per-lane split policy, so whatever is agreed is enforced in integer cents and shown to him
line by line. **No opaque "net."**

## What we ask

1. Permission to build EDF and store products from the episodes he chooses.
2. Help reading records for THYLORA's own family research. A paid consulting rate is offered
   separately and isn't tied to anything else.
3. Credit both ways.

## Pilot (30 days, no lock-in)

- Week 1: one call; he picks 3 existing videos.
- Week 2: we deliver the edit pass plus a records pack for each.
- Week 3: one EDF edition and one store product go live with his approval.
- Week 4: review the numbers together. Continue, change or stop. He keeps everything we made for him.
