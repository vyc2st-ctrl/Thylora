## E. Page architecture — native, not a broadsheet

`THY-EDEREARIAH-NEWS-JOB-001` prohibits *"generic Earth broadsheet imitation as default"* and
*"detached headline/body structure when the story is better carried by map, person, timeline,
question, or local consequence."*

**What is removed:** the masthead-above-columns hierarchy, the headline-then-body split, the
multi-column grid, the dateline, the jump. Those solve an Earth problem — selling one sheet of paper
to strangers at a distance — that this object does not have.

**What replaces it:** a **wayfinding plate**. The organising question is not *what is the news* but
*where am I standing and what does this change here*. Eight zones, asymmetrical, one dominant path.

| Zone | Job | Weight | Why it is placed there |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1 ORIENTATION BAND** | World · settlement · district · time-region · desk · correspondent | 10% | First, always, and it reads across the full width like a signpost. A resident must place themselves before anything else. **Unfilled fields print as UNRESOLVED, visibly.** |
| **2 LOCAL STORY CORE** | What happened here, in one held statement | 16% | Large, left, but **not centred** — it is a statement made to neighbours, not a headline shouted at strangers. |
| **3 HUMAN THREAD** | The household, the day, the table | 22% | The widest single zone. In this system the person *is* the structure, not the illustration of it. |
| **4 EVIDENCE WINDOW** | Documented / inference / unknown, kept apart | 14% | **Offset right, inset, edged.** It reads as a window cut into the page — deliberately not a sidebar, because a sidebar is optional and this is not. |
| **5 UNDERSTANDING BLOCK** | `P_solve = L × M × S` with all three meanings | 14% | Directly beneath the human thread, because it explains what just happened at that table rather than floating as theory. |
| **6 WHAT CHANGES HERE** | Consequence, each item classified | 12% | The zone an Earth paper does not have at all. |
| **7 NEXT QUESTION** | The single open question | 4% | Small, and last before the record. The paper ends on a question, not a moral. |
| **8 WORLD MEMORY** | The ledger entry this event becomes | 8% | A record strip, not a footer — it looks like something written *into* a book. |

**One dominant path:** ORIENTATION → CORE → HUMAN THREAD → UNDERSTANDING → WHAT CHANGES → QUESTION
→ MEMORY, with the EVIDENCE WINDOW held to the side so it can be consulted at any point without
breaking the path. That is the asymmetry, and it is functional.

story tells, and the difference stays unpublished.
