## 4 · Serial and provenance records — the record system `[S]` on `[E]` patterns

| R6 | **Class stock book** — Mrs Harrowell's own account of what Class III received and what it issued | Class teacher | `[E→S] S-14` |
| R7 | **Requisition and order file** — annual requisition to the authority, countersigned by the correspondent | Correspondent, Mr Prile | `[E→S] S-05` |
| R8 | **Delivery notes and invoices** | Head | `[S]` |
| R9 | **Managers' minute book** | Correspondent | `[E→S] S-06` |

| R11 | **Council salvage weight book** — material, weight, date, yard | **Walter Noakes**, UDC | `[E→S] S-19` |

**The rule that makes the episode possible `[E] S-10`:** the log book cannot be
corrected by erasure. **A mistake stays visible and is answered by a later
entry.** The show's method — never delete, always reconcile — is not invented
for it. It is the law the real record was kept under. It is also, deliberately,
the same rule this production applied to the `WICK → WYCK` spelling
(`BIBLE-EP001.md` §1.2).

### 7.1 · Why C-11 is last

Not because it is unlikely — under `S-12` it is entirely plausible — but because
**the episode's rule is that a person is the last hypothesis, not the first.**
C-01 to C-10 are all explanations in which **nobody did anything wrong.** Nine of
them are ordinary consequences of a school running on controlled paper in a year
when its own paperwork is being rewritten.

**WYCK's behaviour here is the load-bearing beat:** when a child first says a
name, **WYCK goes quiet** (`W-6`), and Bramble notices the quiet before he
notices the name.

### 7.2 · What this bible does **not** establish

- **Which candidate is true.** `U-01`.
- Whether the twelfth book is ever recovered, or whether the episode ends with a
  **corrected record instead of a recovered object** — which is the thematically
  stronger ending and is therefore **explicitly not chosen here** so that the
  Chairman chooses it. `U-01a`.
- Whether any character is culpable. **No character in this bible is written as
  guilty.** Any later draft that makes one guilty must be written against this
  file, not over it.
