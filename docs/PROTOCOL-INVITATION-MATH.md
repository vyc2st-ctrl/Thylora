# PROTOCOL · Math in Every Invitation

**Status:** LOCKED by the Chairman · 2026-10-03
**Scope:** Every invitation the Chairman sends, to anyone: family, partners, events, members.

## Rule

1. Every invitation carries **at least one line of math** that says something true
   about the invitation or the person invited: a distance, a count, a time, a proof.
2. The math must be **correct**.
3. The math must be **explained in one plain sentence** directly under it.
4. Short is fine. One line of real math beats a page of decoration.

## Example

> lim₍d → 0₎ W(d) = W_max
> *As the distance between us goes to zero, my well-being goes to its maximum. RSVP by sitting next to me.*

## Companion rule (family missions)

Every mission card given to a family member carries math they must use to finish it.
The loop is always: **Mission Card → assistant → SEND-BACK block → next Mission Card.**

## Privacy note

This repository is public. Personal letters, children's details, printer addresses
and device codes are **not** stored here.
