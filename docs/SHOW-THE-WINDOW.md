# THE WINDOW — live countdown show (working title)

**The idea:** MTV's *TRL* energy — a glass studio over a downtown Manhattan
street, a crowd outside holding signs, a daily countdown — but every video is
from **our people on Earth**, and every creator gets paid transparently through
RAE Link.

**Recommended name: THE WINDOW.** It names the thing people remember (the studio
window over the street), it isn't TRL, and it reads as "a window onto us".
Alternates: *Total Roots Live* · *Request the Block* · *Downtown Countdown*.

## Format (60 minutes, daily)

| Segment | What happens | Lane it feeds |
|---|---|---|
| **The Countdown** | Top 10 Earth videos voted by RAE Link viewers that day | RAE Link views + votes |
| **First Play** | One new video premieres in the window | Creator Studio publication gate |
| **Where You From?** | Crowd shout-outs: a viewer names a town and a great-grandparent | The Root House |
| **Grandma's Tea** | Five-minute sit-down with an elder about a song, a town, a memory | Family Story Partnership |
| **Who Got Paid** | On-screen: what the top video earned and how it split | RAE Link ledger (no hidden percentage) |

## Rules it inherits from RAE Link

- Earth channels are real people; world (EdereAriah) channels are labelled simulated.
- Nothing airs without passing the rights gate. **Music videos need licences from
  the label and publisher** — that is the main real-world blocker for a
  countdown show and is held until a music-rights provider is chosen.
- Children appear only with recorded guardian consent.

## Held

- Music licensing provider (label + publishing + performance rights).
- Real studio location vs. in-world set. In-world first; a real downtown window is a lease decision.
- Live-stream provider (one of the 16 open RAE Link capability decisions).
