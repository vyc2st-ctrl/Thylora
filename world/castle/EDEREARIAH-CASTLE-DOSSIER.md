## 2 · Earth mirror — Windsor structural spine (`S1`)

The facts EdereAriah's castle may closely match.

### 2.1 Founding and site
- Founded by William the Conqueror **c. 1070**, complete about sixteen years
  later; sited high above the south bank of the Thames on the edge of a Saxon
  hunting ground, to guard the **western approach to London**. (W1)
- Occupies **13 acres (≈5 hectares)**. (W11/X1)
- **Oldest and largest occupied castle in the world**; home to 1,000 years of
  royal history; **40 monarchs**. (W11, X1)
- Original form: motte-and-bailey, **timber walls**. (W1)

### 2.2 The three wards
| Ward | Function |
|---|---|
| **Lower Ward** | Public / official face. St George's Chapel, College of St George, Horseshoe Cloister, Curfew Tower. Henry II's public State residence sat here. (W1, C1) |
| **Middle Ward** | The motte and the **Round Tower**. Today houses the **Royal Archives** and **Royal Photograph Collection**. (W1, W11) |
| **Upper Ward** | The Quadrangle, **State Apartments**, **Semi-State Rooms**, **private apartments**. Henry II's smaller private residence stood on the north side. (W1, W3) |

### 2.3 Build phases (`S1`)

## 4 · Room and building character — mirror standard

Detailed map: `ROOM-BUILDING-MAP.md`.

**State Apartment character (`S1`, Windsor):** Baroque state rooms of the
1675–84 campaign — illusionistic painted ceilings, deep limewood carving,
gilding — **overlaid** by a Georgian-Gothic remodel of the 1820s that re-cut
the exterior and much of the interior into a romantic register. The result is
**two manners in one building**, not one. This doubling is the single most
characteristic thing about the mirror and should be carried into EdereAriah.

**Private-room character (`S1`):** the Semi-State Rooms — created for George IV,
in use as private apartments, richer and more intimate than the State Rooms
(W3, W16). Crimson Drawing Room, Green Drawing Room, White Drawing Room.

**Chapel architecture (`S1`):** Perpendicular Gothic, stone fan/crossing vault
completed long after the walls, heraldic display of the order's knights in the
vault and the quire stalls, chantry chapels, royal burials. (C1, C2, C3)

**Service circulation (`S1`):** the kitchen sits **inside the Upper Ward** near
the State and private apartments, so food travels a short protected internal
route to the royal table. See `ROYAL-KITCHEN.md` §6.
