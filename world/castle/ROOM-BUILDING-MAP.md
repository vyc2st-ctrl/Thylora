## 7 · Circulation rules carried from the mirror

1. **Two entries, never one.** Ceremonial entry through the lower/public ward;
   goods and staff through a separate service entrance. They do not cross.
2. **Kitchen inside the seat.** The Great Kitchen sits within the Upper Ward,
   not in an outbuilding, so the food route to the royal table is short,
   internal and protected.
3. **Ward hierarchy is a privacy gradient.** Public → memory/archive →
   private seat. Access narrows at every gate.
4. **Two manners in one fabric.** Baroque state interior inside a
   Georgian-Gothic exterior. Any EdereAriah room description must declare
   which layer it belongs to.
5. **The chapel is a separate jurisdiction.** Its own college, its own clergy,
   its own establishment — inside the walls, not under the household.
