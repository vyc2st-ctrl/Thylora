Travel through time. Prepare for the era. Help more than you take.

ERA CONDITIONS

Use the time you were given.

Money, roads, vehicles, laws, available tools, communications and travel conditions follow the assigned era. Exact event years remain open until sealed for a run.

SAFE SPEED

Fast only when the context is safe.

Speed never outranks a person in danger. Emergency response, health, crime context and local conditions can change the route or stop competition priority.

HOST COMMUNITIES

Invitation is a privilege.

Homes and communities can host participants. Invitation does not create ownership, entitlement or permission to exploit local people or resources.

CROWN ROLE

Caretakers, not rulers.

The Crown administers the event for the whole community. The event is designed to build trust and real relationships without pretending every person or place is ideal.
