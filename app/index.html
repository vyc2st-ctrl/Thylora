FAMILY STORY ARCHIVE

The original stays visible. Everybody else adds a layer.

A family can preserve what one person said, then let siblings, children and later generations add their own remembered version or interpretation without overwriting the source story.

01 · SOURCE STORYWhat the teller actually said, as preserved.
02 · FAMILY PERSPECTIVES“This is how I remember hearing it.” Each person's version stays attributed.
03 · ERSATZ INTERPRETATIONDramatized or comedic adaptation. Invented dialogue stays here.
04 · EVIDENCEPublic records, photos, dates, places and UNKNOWNs.
Archive access

Leaving the app can end a person's access without automatically deleting other authorized relatives' family archive. A contributor can request removal of their own material; the system tracks scope, family impact, active-copy deletion and completion evidence.
